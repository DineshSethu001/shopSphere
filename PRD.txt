## 1. Product Overview

ShopSphere is a full-stack e-commerce platform designed to provide customers with a seamless online shopping experience and administrators with an efficient product, order, coupon and inventory management system.

The platform allows customers to browse products, search and filter products, manage shopping carts, place orders, make online payments, track purchases, submit reviews and manage wishlists.

Administrators can manage products, customers' orders, inventory, coupons and business analytics through a secure dashboard.

## 2. Product Objectives

- Develop a responsive and user-friendly e-commerce platform.
- Provide secure customer registration and authentication.
- Implement efficient product discovery and shopping cart functionality.
- Support Cash on Delivery (COD) and Razorpay online payments.
- Provide centralized product and order management.
- Maintain accurate inventory and order records.
- Optimize website performance, security and SEO.
- Deploy the application using cloud infrastructure.

## 7. System Architecture

The application follows a client-server architecture.

""Frontend:""
React.js communicates with backend REST APIs using Axios.

""Backend:""
Express.js handles business logic, authentication, product management, orders and payments.

""Database:""
MongoDB stores customer information, products, orders, coupons and reviews.

""External Services:""
- Cloudinary for image storage.
- Razorpay for online payments.
- Vercel for frontend hosting.
- Render for backend hosting.

## 8. Database Design

Primary MongoDB collections:

| Collection | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Users | Customer and admin information |
| Products | Product details and inventory |
| Orders | Customer order information |
| Coupons | Discount management |
| Reviews | Product ratings and feedback |

### Important Relationships
- One customer can have multiple orders.
- One product can appear in multiple orders.
- One product can have multiple reviews.
- One customer can maintain a wishlist.
- Orders reference customers and products.

## 9. API Requirements

### Authentication
- POST /api/auth/register
- POST /api/auth/login

### Products
- GET /api/products
- GET /api/products/:id
- GET /api/products/categories
- POST /api/products
- PUT /api/products/:id
- DELETE /api/products/:id

### Orders
- POST /api/orders
- GET /api/orders/my-orders
- GET /api/orders/my-orders/:id
- POST /api/orders/my-orders/:id/cancel

### Wishlist
- GET /api/wishlist
- POST /api/wishlist
- DELETE /api/wishlist/:productId

### Coupons
- POST /api/coupons/validate
- POST /api/coupons
- GET /api/coupons

### Payments
- POST /api/payments/create-order
- POST /api/payments/verify

### Inventory
- GET /api/admin/inventory
- PUT /api/admin/inventory/:id

*All protected and administrative endpoints must enforce the appropriate authentication and authorization rules.*
